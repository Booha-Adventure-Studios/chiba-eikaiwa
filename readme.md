
# 千葉英会話ガイド — Chiba Eikaiwa Guide

**The best guide to independent English schools across Chiba Prefecture, Japan.**

🌐 Live site: [booha-adventure-studios.github.io/chiba-eikaiwa](https://booha-adventure-studios.github.io/chiba-eikaiwa/)

---

## What this is

A clean, free directory of owner-run English conversation schools across Chiba Prefecture. No chains, no rankings, no paid placements, no email required.

Schools are organized by area and city. A featured school rotates randomly on every visit. Each listing links directly to the school's own website.

Built and maintained by [Bryan Harper](https://www.bryanharper.tokyo), an English teacher based in Tomisato, Chiba.

---

## Who it's for

- Families in Chiba looking for a local, independent English school
- Adults looking for conversation classes outside of chain schools
- Teachers and school owners who want to be listed

## For schools — how to get listed

Listing is free. There are no fees, no rankings, no strings.

To be listed you need:
- An owner-run school in Chiba Prefecture
- A working website or contact page
- A photo or logo
- Four bullet points on why someone should join your school

Contact: [bryans.english@gmail.com](mailto:bryans.english@gmail.com)
